01

Research

Sources first: the building, the city and the medicine of 1206.

The complex dates to 1205–06: a hospital and a medical school side by side, built by Sultan Gıyaseddin Keyhüsrev I after the will of his sister Gevher Nesibe.

Read more

The hospital stands on the west, the Gıyasiye medical school on the east; both have an open courtyard with four iwans and a pool. The hospital had vaulted patient rooms, and no dome and no glazed tiles.

For the medicine of the time we followed the sources: diagnosis by pulse and by the glass flask, diet, cupping and remedies like theriac, taught at the bedside and then discussed in the great iwan. Anything the sources could not confirm stayed out of the film.

04

Blender model

A grey model built from the survey plans holds the architecture.

We built the whole complex as a grey block model in Blender from the published survey plans, with 23 fixed cameras. The model never appears in the film; it keeps walls, arches and openings in place in every shot.

Read more

The model also fixes the real orientation. The gate faces south and Mount Erciyes stands about 22 km to the south, so the mountain only enters the frame from the north or from above. Five cameras on the plan are read straight from the Blender file; the others are placed by hand from their frames.

06

AI frames

The model locks the architecture; AI adds light, stone, cloth and people.

Each frame was generated on top of a grey render. Every character came from a fixed design sheet, so the same faces return from shot to shot.

Read more

AI kept adding things from later centuries, so every frame was checked: no domes or tiles on the hospital, none of Kayseri's later monuments on the skyline, no illustrated anatomy books, no Asclepius staff, books stacked flat as they were. The city stays modest, because no source gives its size in 1206.

07

One palette

“Çini Turquoise”: cool shadows; warm stone, skin and copper.

Three palettes were tested on the same frame and T2 was chosen: turquoise in the shadows and mid-tones, while sunlit stone, copper, wood and skin stay warm. No purple, no pink.

Read more

An image model was used only as a colour guide. Its colour was transferred onto the original pixels, region by region, so the fine texture of stone and cloth stays exactly as it was.

Frame to motion

Kling 3.0 Pro, chosen after testing eleven video models.

Approved frames became video. We picked the model after testing eleven video models on the same first and last frames in September 2026.

09

The star and the serpents

A Seljuk star in the stone turns into the faculty's two serpents.

The faculty's emblem of two serpents comes from a carved panel on this gate, a motif Seljuk builders used as a talisman against illness. The motion was drawn first as six poses, then animated.
