How it was made

It started with research and survey drawings: plans, sections and the carved gate. From those, a grey Blender model of the whole complex, so every shot has a real camera position. Each frame was generated with AI on top of that model, graded in one palette of Seljuk turquoise against warm stone, and only then turned into video.
