Benefits

What sponsorship gets you

01

Viewers looking for material

Viewers reach us by typing things like "grade 9 religious culture 1st exam" or "KPSS answers". At that moment they are choosing what to study with. That is why the book we recommend reads as part of the answer, not as an ad.

02

Visible across the whole archive

Our 2,600+ videos are searched and watched again every exam season. Your sponsor link sits not only on new uploads but across an archive people have been watching for years.

03

The decision makers watch too

34% of viewers are aged 35–54: the parents and teachers who pay for books and courses. You reach the student and the person paying in the same video.

04

You see the results in a report

Every campaign gets its own discount code and link. At the end of the period we send a report with views, clicks and code redemptions. A renewed contract is our goal too.

What doesn't fit these channels

Part of our audience is 13–17 and trusts us. That is why we only work with education-related brands.

By category

  • Games, betting and gambling
  • Crypto, investment and credit products
  • Homework-for-hire and cheating apps
  • Services selling subscribers, views or followers
  • Political parties, candidates and campaigns

By model

  • Product-only (barter) deals
  • Requests that change lesson content
  • Publishing without an #ad label
  • Verbal deals without a contract
