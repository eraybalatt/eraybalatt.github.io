Overview

FLAWLESS is a cinematic animated documentary reconstructing the 2003 Antwerp Diamond Heist — over $100 million stolen from a vault the world called impossible to breach, without a single alarm.

It is the debut episode of CITRINE, an original true-crime documentary channel I created, wrote, directed, edited and narrated. What sets it apart from every other retelling: a first-person account from Leonardo Notarbartolo himself — the leader of the crew — woven directly into the reconstruction.

The Challenge

Launch a brand-new channel with a first episode that could stand next to established true-crime studios — cinematic, historically faithful, and gripping enough to hold viewers for seventeen minutes — while securing something no documentary on this story had before: the voice of the actual thief.

The Approach
  • Story & script. A retention-first narrative — cold open + five acts + outro — engineered around the central irony: a perfect crime undone by an ordinary human mistake.
  • Original interview. Conducted and integrated an exclusive interview with Leonardo Notarbartolo (in Italian, fully subtitled) as the emotional spine of the film.
  • Reconstruction. 30+ bespoke 3D scenes rebuilding the vault, the sensors and the escape — art-directed in a signature "Amber-Noir" look.
  • Motion & identity. 35 custom motion graphics (security counters, forensic overlays, date stamps, the DNA reveal) plus an original title sequence, opener and outro.
  • Sound & grade. Professional narration, full sound design and music, mixed to broadcast loudness (−14 LUFS), finished with a cinematic color grade.
  • Localization. English, Turkish and Italian subtitles — plus a complete Italian dub in my own voice-cloned narration. German, Spanish and French in production.

The Centerpiece — An Interview With the Mastermind

The heart of the film is an exclusive conversation with the man who led the heist. Rather than narrate about him, the documentary lets Notarbartolo speak for himself — the planning, the moment the vault opened, the single mistake, and how he sees it today — turning a reconstruction into first-person history.

Localization & Reach

Beyond subtitles, FLAWLESS was localized with a full Italian-language dub generated in my own cloned voice — fitting, since the story is Italian and Notarbartolo's original interview is already in Italian. The pipeline extends to the highest-value markets, with German, Spanish and French versions underway, and multi-language audio tracks on a single upload.
