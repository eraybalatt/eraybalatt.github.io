Why work gets rejected

The five failures I fix

These are the reasons AI content gets scrapped after the first review round. Every shot I deliver passes two checks: technical consistency, and whether it still feels real inside the finished sequence.

  • Characters changing between shots — a different face in every cut
  • Products warping or losing the details that matter
  • UGC that feels too polished and reads as obviously synthetic
  • Lip sync and localisation that break the original performance
  • Clips that look good alone but fall apart as a complete edit
How it runs

From brief to delivery

You get a fixed quote before anything starts, and a named milestone at every stage — no hourly surprises.

  1. Brief & fixed quote

    You send the product, the goal and any reference. I come back with what today's models can reliably deliver, what I would change, and a fixed price. 1–2 days.

  2. Look development

  3. Production

    Full generation, editing, motion, sound design, score and colour. You see a first cut and get revision rounds inside the quote. 1–2 weeks.

  4. Delivery

    Master files in every aspect ratio you need, plus platform variants and language versions. Commercial rights transfer with delivery. Same week.

FAQ

Before you write

What does an AI brand film cost?

UGC and short-form ad packages start at $1,500. Product and brand films run $3,000–$8,000 depending on the number of films, products and characters. Music videos run $2,500–$6,000. Documentaries are quoted per project. Every project is quoted as a fixed price before work starts.

How long does production take?

UGC and short-form ad sets take 1–2 weeks. Brand and product films take 2–3 weeks. A full campaign has been delivered in 72 hours when the brief was locked — three films, six products, two consistent characters and an original score.

Do you need a film crew, actors or a studio?

No. There is no live-action camera photography, no casting, no location and no studio rental. Everything is produced through a custom generative AI pipeline and finished in Premiere Pro and After Effects.

Can you keep the same character across every shot?

Yes — this is the core of the workflow. One music video for Universal Music Türkiye held a single character across more than 40 shots, and a brand campaign held two synthetic characters across three separate films.

Can you localise an existing ad into another language?

Yes. An existing master ad can be rebuilt in another language with the same performer, the same delivery and true lip sync — no reshoot. One master ad was rebuilt in English, German and Portuguese this way.

Who owns the finished films?

You do. Commercial usage rights for the delivered films transfer to the client, and licensing terms are written into the quote before production starts.

Which tools do you use?

The pipeline changes as the models change. Currently: Veo, Kling, Seedance, Runway, Nano Banana and Midjourney for image and video, ElevenLabs for voice, Suno for original music, finished in Premiere Pro and After Effects.
