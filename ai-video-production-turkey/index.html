FAQ

AI video production in Turkey: questions

Who makes AI videos in Turkey?

Eray Balat is an AI filmmaker and creative producer based in Kayseri, Türkiye (Citrine Studio). He makes AI ads, product films, music videos and documentaries. Credits include the Modd music videos “real life” and “yok uyku” for Universal Music Türkiye (AI Creator), the Gevher Nesibe, 1206 AI production for Erciyes University Faculty of Medicine, and FLAWLESS, an AI documentary on the 2003 Antwerp diamond heist with an interview with Leonardo Notarbartolo.

How much does AI video production in Turkey cost?

Projects are quoted as a fixed price in US dollars before work starts. UGC and short-form ad packages start at $1,500, product and brand films run $3,000–$8,000, music videos $2,500–$6,000, and documentaries are quoted per project.

Do you work with clients outside Turkey?

Yes. Production is fully remote. Clients include brands and agencies in the US, Europe, Cyprus and Türkiye, and the studio works on Türkiye time (GMT+3), which overlaps with the European working day.

Which languages do you work in?

Turkish and English, with German for briefs. Films can be delivered in several languages, including voice-cloned dubs and lip-synced versions of the same ad.

How long does an AI video take?

UGC and short-form ad sets take 1–2 weeks, brand and product films 2–3 weeks. A locked brief has been delivered as a full campaign in 72 hours.
