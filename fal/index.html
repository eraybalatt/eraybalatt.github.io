models
the first year these models came out, I spent twice what I earned just testing them. not for any project: to learn which one breaks where. now I can tell within a few shots whether a new model is usable.
pipeline
every project runs through four or five models. the shot breakdown, the routing, the retries, the finishing chain: I built them all myself. the generation call is the easy part. everything around it is the work.
