The brief

Four problems in one frame

Alphan İnşaat wanted two Instagram ads for its project in Aydınlıkevler, Kayseri: one for the 3 detached blocks, one for the 4 terraced blocks. Each had to stop the scroll in the first second, show the buildings finished and carry the campaign. Four things stood in the way.

The building isn't there yet

On shoot day the site was an empty plot and a concrete frame. The product being sold wasn't in the picture.

The architecture can't drift

AI models redraw a building a little differently every time. What the client sells is that façade, that floor count, that plot.

The location's ace: the tram

A tram line runs right past the project. AI kept putting the tram in the wrong lane, at the wrong size, and broke the building on the way.

30 seconds, vertical

Finished building, location, interiors, payment plan, price and contact, all readable on a phone screen and all inside 30 seconds.

Before and after

Same frame. Before the building, and after.

Left of each pair is what the camera really saw on 9 November 2026; right is what the client is selling. Angle, street and skyline stay the same. Only the building changes, and it changes into the architect's design, not a guess.

The pairs are not pixel-identical because the after shots move, so they sit side by side rather than under a slider. In the drone shot, the advertising wrap already on the passing tram was replaced with Alphan's own.

How we built it

A hybrid pipeline, built for accuracy

A sales film can't afford AI's habit of redrawing a building a little differently every time: the floor count drifts, the façade changes, the building slides off its plot. So the work was set up the way a visual effects shot is set up, with the real footage and the real architecture as fixed points and AI working between them.

Live action on location

Half a day on site: 11 drone and 15 ground takes at 50 fps, the ground camera turned on its side for native vertical framing. The developer's representative speaks the opening and closing lines to camera, and the voice-over is the same voice, so the ad sounds like the company itself. Camera and drone: Fatih Bozkırlı.

Architecture first, in 3D

We rebuilt the developer's 3D model in Blender, completed the missing blocks from their renders and took every storey height from the floor plans. Then the model was matched to the camera of the real drone footage, so the building stands on its true plot, at its true height, before AI touches a single pixel. Most exterior shots were generated from these guide frames.

AI on rails

Finished façades, pavement and tram-stop life, interiors and the shift from day to night were generated with AI, held to the developer's 13 renders and the plans of 5 apartment types. AI adds light and life; it never decides where a wall goes.

3D wherever AI can't be trusted

The tram is the project's strongest selling point, and AI put it in the side lane instead of on its grass track, drew it too big and changed the building on the way. So we modelled it at real scale, solved the camera of each AI shot frame by frame and composited the tram onto its own rails.

Behind the scenes · 1

The building, locked to reality

Left alone, AI redraws the building on every generation. To stop that, the developer's model was built in Blender and matched to the camera of the real drone footage. AI then worked from these guide frames, so the building's position, height and floor count stayed tied to the real thing.

Behind the scenes · 2

The tram, built instead of generated

The approved AI shot was never touched. The tram was rendered as a separate 3D layer and composited on top, so the architecture the client signed off on stays exactly as it was.

Camera solve

The camera motion of each AI shot was solved frame by frame, tracking kerb lines and pole bases to half-pixel accuracy.

The right line

The poles were measured and the rails laid with at least half a metre of clearance, so no tram ever runs through a pole.

Real scale

Modelled to the dimensions of Kayseri's tram: 32.6 m long, 2.65 m wide, as single and double sets.

Render

Rendered in Cycles with motion blur and a shadow on the ground, passing correctly behind the poles, wires and trees in front of it.

Night

Lit interiors, passenger silhouettes in the windows, a darker roof and reflections, with no false shadow on the road.

Flexibility

Day and night versions, different framings and line-ups. After review the tram runs more often: in three scenes, and with double sets on both lines at night.

Why it mattersAI is fast, but it picks the position and size of a moving object on its own. In a sales film, a tram in the wrong lane or running through a pole breaks trust. The hybrid route pairs the speed of AI with the precision of 3D.
